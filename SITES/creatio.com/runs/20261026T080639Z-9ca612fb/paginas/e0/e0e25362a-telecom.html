Marketing & Promotion

Lead management

Improve lead-to-customer conversions by automating the entire lead management lifecycle – from the first contact to hand-off to sales.

Multichannel campaign management

Run personalized campaigns that match subscriber preferences in terms of channels, time, and content.

Marketing resource management

Manage diverse marketing workflows, information, and content in a single system to better align your marketing team and streamline execution of new campaigns and programs.

Loyalty program management

Implement telco loyalty programs with an omnichannel approach to engage subscribers at every step of their journey and reduce churn rates.

Sales & Distribution

Account and contact management

Get a comprehensive view of a subscriber's personal, behavioral, buying, and spending details to personalize customer engagement and improve sales efficiency.

Opportunity management

Increase win rates by automating workflows for different types of telecom sales opportunities (B2B, B2C, and wholesale).

Partner relationship management

Drive partner sales and onboarding through a portal that supports leads, opportunities, collaboration, mutual campaigns, knowledge sharing, and loyalty programs.

Sales planning and forecasting

Create and manage forecasts in multiple dimensions – by teams, territories, telecom products, and other parameters.

Cross-selling and upselling

Optimize customer lifetime value by cross-selling and upselling different telecom products that your subscribers really need.

Quotes, Orders and Invoices

Product information management

Get a comprehensive view and simplified access to up-to-date information about all your telecom products and services to support sales, ordering, billing and other processes.

Quotation management

Quickly prepare error-free, attractive quotes based on up-to-date, centralized product information.

Customer order management

Streamline management of cross-channel customer orders using a single interface and intelligent workflows for order processing.

Invoicing

Produce invoices instantly, monitor their status, and ensure comprehensive oversight of the payment flow.

Infrastructure management and Operations

Network rollout management

Efficiently manage network planning, design, and deployment activities using advanced project management and collaboration tools.

Procurement and supply chain management

Apply collaborative and AI-powered workflows to streamline complex processes related to planning, purchasing, storing, and transporting network equipment.

RFx management and contracting

Easily create contracts and track their status using collaboration tools and guided workflows to ensure that employees, vendors, and customers comply with the stated requirements.

Asset management

Get quick, centralized access to information about all network-related assets and their lifecycle to effectively plan, manage, and optimize network rollout projects.

Internal service management

Regulatory compliance control

Legal case management

Certification development

Internal audit
