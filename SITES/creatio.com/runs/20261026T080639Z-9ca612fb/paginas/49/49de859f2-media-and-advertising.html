Marketing

Segmentation

Establish flexible customer segmentation based on diverse criteria, empowering the implementation of targeted and personalized marketing strategies that resonate effectively with specific audience segments.

Campaign management

Leverage an integrated system to plan, execute, and optimize campaigns of any complexity and scale, resulting in more impactful and data-driven marketing strategies.

Lead management

Ensure a streamlined and unified approach to converting prospects into valuable customers with a centralized platform for efficient lead tracking, nurturing, and communication.

Account-based marketing (ABM)

Enhance ABM initiatives by taking advantage of targeted and personalized communication, automated account segmentation, and data-driven insights, maximizing the engagement of high-value customers.

Event marketing

Run successful and impactful marketing events by using tools for seamless event planning, attendee engagement tracking, and post-event analysis.

Sales

Opportunity management

Ensure a more streamlined and effective process for identifying, pursuing, and closing valuable opportunities by using ready-made and flexible sales workflows, centralized information, and collaborative activity management.

Cross-selling and upselling

Enhance upselling and cross-selling strategies by leveraging insights into customer preferences, purchase history, and behavior. Deliver targeted and personalized recommendations that boost revenue opportunities as well as foster long-term customer relationships.

Contract management

Centralize contracts, agreements, and related documents in a unified database and speed up processing time via automation of contract approvals and other document management workflows.

Partner relationship management

Manage partnerships and subcontractor relationships via an advanced partner portal that streamlines joint lead management, campaign coordination, and knowledge sharing.

Invoicing

Effortlessly generate and send invoices to the customer for the services provided, ensuring accurate billing and financial reconciliation.

Sales performance analysis

Analyze sales data and performance metrics to identify trends, assess the effectiveness of sales strategies, and make data-driven improvements.

Customer onboarding

Customer 360

Have a comprehensive and unified view of the customer's interactions, preferences, and history to personalize onboarding experiences, anticipate needs, and create a more efficient onboarding journey.

Customer lifecycle management

Ensure a smooth and efficient journey from initial contact to account setup and beyond by centralizing information, automating processes, and enabling personalized interactions.

Account management

Streamline account setup with a centralized platform for efficient communication, task automation, and real-time data access. Assign dedicated account managers as the main point of contact to oversee campaign execution.

Data and asset management

Leverage a centralized platform for efficient collection and management of the entire scope of customer information and branding assets, maximizing productivity and ensuring seamless collaboration among teams throughout the onboarding process.

Project management

Resource planning and management

Create a comprehensive project plan within a unified system, incorporating timelines, budget, resource allocation, and communication strategies to guarantee the success and timely delivery of the campaign.

Team collaboration

Effectively coordinate with various teams, such as designers, copywriters, and production specialists, in a single environment and across multiple projects. Share content, ideas, and knowledge quickly and easily.

Budget allocation

Determine the budget and allocate it across different media channels based on their reach, relevance, and cost-effectiveness.

Project monitoring and reporting

Monitor the progress of the campaign in real-time, track key performance indicators, and generate regular reports for both internal teams and customers.

Ad production and placement

Media planning and buying

Choose relevant media channels, negotiate and secure advertising space, and communicate effectively with colleagues, customers, and subcontractors across diverse channels within a unified environment.

Production management

Develop a detailed plan for the production process, outlining tasks, timelines, and resource requirements. Coordinate with relevant teams to ensure a smooth production workflow.

Documentation and archiving

Use a single system to document the production process, including feedback, revisions, and final versions. Archive all project-related materials for future reference or re-use.

Customer service and support

Proactive issue resolution

Effectively serve and engage customers through various communication channels using an integrated digital customer service workspace, AI-driven workflow automation, and a comprehensive 360-degree customer view.

Feedback collection and iteration

Actively collect feedback from customers at various stages of the project, iterating on creative concepts and making adjustments based on customer input.

Customer self-service

Provide customers with a user-friendly portal for streamlined request registration, easy access to a comprehensive knowledge base, and efficient interaction, thereby reducing the workload on the service department.

Risk and compliance

Customer privacy management

Implement personal data compliance workflows in accordance with the industry’s best practices and data privacy regulations.

Regulatory compliance control

Ensure regulatory compliance across the organization via centralized management of regulatory documents, compliance plans, employee policies, etc.

Legal case management

Streamline management of legal cases, ensure unified format for documents, and optimize legal-related workflows and collaboration processes.

Certification development

Monitor and manage certification processes throughout the entire organization to ensure complete regulatory compliance.

Internal audit

Manage audit workflows across all the required financial, physical, and legal assets within your organization.

Employee experience

Employee lifecycle management

Implement unified and transparent HR workflows for efficient employee management, including hiring, onboarding, development, retention, and exit.

Employee request management

Improve the employee experience by providing a user-friendly solution to automate all types of requests (vacation, travel, expenses, etc.).

Collaboration

Consolidate communications, collaborate, share knowledge and documents, plan your tasks and meetings, and manage reviews and approvals with maximum efficiency.
